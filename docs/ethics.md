# Ethics

- Obtain informed consent for conversational data collection.
- Avoid consequential, medical, legal, or real financial decisions in pilot scenarios.
- Clearly label investment and business scenarios as simulations.
- Minimize and de-identify data.
- Separate consent records from research artifacts.
- Do not submit participant-identifying text to AI providers.
- Report null and negative findings.
- Distinguish exploratory and preregistered analyses.
- Obtain institutional or independent review when required.
