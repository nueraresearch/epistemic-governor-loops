# HAM Topologies

This notation represents research hypotheses, not established system taxonomies.

## Candidate topologies

- **H+A:** Human and AI interact directly.
- **H+A+G:** Human and AI interact through one or more governance gates.
- **H+AA:** Human interacts with an AI system represented by multiple artificial-agent roles.
- **H+AA+G:** Governed multi-agent interaction between human and artificial participants.
- **HH+A:** Multiple humans interact with one AI.
- **HH+AA:** Multiple humans and multiple artificial-agent roles interact.
- **HH+AA+G:** Multi-human, multi-agent interaction with explicit governance.

## Interpretation

`A` means an AI role, not necessarily one model instance. `AA` means a multiplicity or collection of roles and must not be conflated with count of deployed agents.

`G` denotes governance or gate structure, not a particular person or agent.

## Measurement questions

For each topology, compare:

- CCR
- ADR
- CAS
- BCE
- creativity
- idea generation
- latency
- disagreement
- correction success
- override frequency
- failure mode

Topologies should not be compared without controlling for task, participant skill, model behavior, interface, and time pressure.
