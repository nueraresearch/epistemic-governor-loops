# Research Agenda

## Project title

**Epistemic Governor Loops: Calibration Dynamics in Human-AI Reasoning Systems**

## Core research question

Can a human and an AI, each possessing distinct reasoning deficiencies, form a coupled feedback system more epistemically calibrated than either participant operating independently?

## Broader architecture question

Can interaction architecture improve calibration through loops and governance gates beyond the capabilities of individual participants?

## Phase 1 — establish the phenomenon

- **RQ1:** Do some users naturally produce more calibrated interactions?
- **RQ2:** Can conversational norms systematically reduce confidence inflation?
- **RQ3:** Can humans detect and correct AI-generated assumption drift?

Deliverable: *Observational Evidence for Epistemic Governor Interaction Patterns in Human-AI Dialogue*.

## Phase 2 — define calibration

CCR, ADR, CAS, and BCE require final coding rules, denominators, worked examples, inter-rater procedures, and preregistration.

## Phase 3 — pilot study

A 20–30 participant signal-detection study across normal, reflection, and governor conditions.

Compare calibration, creativity, and idea generation. This stage is not powered for confirmatory publication.

## Phase 4 — mechanism investigation

Test competing explanations:

1. in-context model adjustment;
2. improved human reasoning;
3. interaction-topology effects;
4. gate-enforced governance effects.

The final explanation is not inferred from participant-level outcomes alone.
