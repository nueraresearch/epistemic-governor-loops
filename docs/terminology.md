# Terminology

- **Epistemic governor:** a participant or protocol that detects and prompts repair of unsupported confidence or assumption drift.
- **Epistemic calibration:** alignment between confidence and evidence quality.
- **Confidence creep:** unjustified movement toward greater certainty.
- **Assumption drift:** unsupported assumptions entering or strengthening a reasoning chain.
- **Bidirectional correction:** repair initiated by either participant or by a governing process.
- **Framework:** the loop, roles, transitions, and feedback structure.
- **Governance:** framework plus gates that admit, block, defer, escalate, or audit transitions.
- **Gate:** a control point with observable inputs and state-transition behavior.
- **Participant:** a role that influences subsequent system state.
- **Tool:** a capability-extending artifact that is not an independent participant in the chosen frame.
- **Topology:** the arrangement of participant roles and gate relationships.
