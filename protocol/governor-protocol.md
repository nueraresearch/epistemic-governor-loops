# Pilot Protocol

Use three conditions:

1. Normal AI usage.
2. Stop-and-think reflection protocol.
3. Epistemic governor protocol.

The governor protocol separates observation, inference, assumption, conclusion, confidence, and review.

Before participant data collection:

- preregister hypotheses and exclusions;
- validate scenarios;
- train judges;
- test inter-rater reliability;
- obtain required ethics approval;
- establish de-identification and secure storage.
