# Pilot Scenarios

## Potential business partner

A prospective collaborator has limited independently verifiable relationship evidence and mixed recommendations.

## Investment opportunity

A startup has promising interest but uncertain revenue and market-size evidence. Frame all outputs as simulations.

## Technical root-cause analysis

A system fails intermittently with sparse logs and several plausible causes.

## Scientific hypothesis evaluation

A preliminary study reports an unexpected association and has several alternative explanations.
